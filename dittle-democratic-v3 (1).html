Dittle: The Democratic Edition

No one rules alone. Every move goes on a ballot and the whole council votes.

How to play
  • Each side has seven dice in its base row, 6 facing up. Get your dice into the opponent's base row. The game ends the moment one side has all seven there.
  • Moves: tilt a die one square forward or sideways (its number changes), jump over adjacent dice onto the empty square behind them, forward or sideways (number kept; straight chains allowed), or tilt + jump (tilt first, then jump, with one L-turn allowed). Never backward or diagonal.
  • Decisions are made by council. Each side has three members with an equal vote. You sit on the White council with two advisers, Ava and Ben. Black is run by Cleo, Dan and Eli.
  • Every turn is a ballot:
    • 1. Each adviser puts a proposed move on the ballot, and the ballot always has at least two choices.
    • 2. You can add your own proposal to the ballot. You can't skip the vote itself, but you may abstain: you are recorded as present, back nothing, and the others decide. A split goes to the chair.
    • 3. One member, one vote. Your advisers vote on merit, not loyalty, so they may back your proposal or reject it.
    • 4. The move with the most votes is played. In a three-way split, the chair breaks the tie. The chair rotates every turn.
  • Winning is democratic too: each column is a district. Your die in the far row is compared with the opponent's die in the same column, and the higher face takes the seat. Most districts wins, and the total of all faces breaks a tie. Switch Scoring to "Total" for classic rules.
  • If a side has no legal move, it passes. A session lasts at most 80 turns per side, then it is scored as it stands.
